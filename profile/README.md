An office suite in your browser — documents, spreadsheets, presentations,
PDF, drawings, notes and live code. No account and no server of ours: your
files stay on your device, unless you choose to share them, sync your
devices or collaborate.

- **Try it:** <https://progressive-web-office.github.io/>
- **Documentation:** <https://progressive-web-office.github.io/docs/>
- **Source:** [progressive-web-office.github.io](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office.github.io)
  — free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later, OpenDocument by default,
  Microsoft Office files read and written too.

Questions, bugs and ideas are welcome in the
[issues](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office.github.io/issues).
